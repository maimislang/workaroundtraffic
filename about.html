About the Author

Mai Mislang is a communications specialist with over 20 years of experience in the public, private, and non-profit sectors. A former Assistant Secretary and Chief of Staff in the Presidential Communications Office of the Philippines under President Benigno Aquino III, she helped shape public narratives at the highest levels of government.

Since 2015, she has been using her expertise in revamping the internal and external communications systems of various organizations.

She is the founder and owner of Rise Artisanal Bread Store, a sourdough microbakery she launched during the pandemic, and performs as the lead singer of The Blue Rats, one of the pioneers of blues music in the Philippines.
