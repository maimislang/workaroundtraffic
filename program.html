What you leave with

Four things you can use on Monday.

This is not a lecture series. You leave with working documents built around your own organization, not generic slides.

01

A role-by-role presence map

A clear view of which roles must be onsite, which can flex, and which can go fully remote — decided against your operations, not against a rule of thumb.

02

A cost-of-commute business case

The numbers that make the case to your board: what the commute is costing your people and your organization, and what a flexible arrangement is projected to return.

03

A 90-day pilot plan

A step-by-step plan to run a real pilot, with the metrics to track, the checkpoints to hold, and the guardrails that keep it fair across roles.

04

A policy template

A policy document you can adapt and adopt — written to be practical, defensible, and aligned with the Telecommuting Act.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is the Unstuck Philippines Flexible Work Pilot?
It is a guided program that helps Philippine employers design and run a flexible work arrangement that holds up in practice. You leave with a role-by-role presence map, a cost-of-commute business case, a 90-day pilot plan, and a policy template you can adapt to your own organization.
Who should attend?
The program is built for HR and people leaders, CEOs and senior executives, and sustainability teams — anyone who has the mandate to change where and how their organization works.
Does our organization need to be fully remote to join?
No. The program does not assume a remote-first company. It helps you decide which roles need to be onsite, which do not, and how to design an arrangement that fits your operations. Fully onsite organizations that are only beginning to explore flexibility are welcome.
What is the time commitment?
The Founding Cohort runs across three full-day sessions. The In-house Workshop is delivered to a single company and is scheduled around your team. Exact dates are shared with waitlist members first.
How is our information protected?
Your information is handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. We use it only to contact you about the Unstuck Philippines Flexible Work Pilot, and we do not share it with third parties.
